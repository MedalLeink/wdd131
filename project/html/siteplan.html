Lagos Street Eats

Site Plan Document

Site Name

Lagos Street Eats

This name reflects the geographic focus (Lagos, Nigeria) and the subject matter (street food culture). It is direct, memorable, and easy to brand. A potential domain could be lagosstreeteats.com.

Site Purpose

Lagos Street Eats is an informational website dedicated to celebrating the vibrant street food culture of Lagos, Nigeria. The site will introduce visitors to popular Nigerian street foods such as suya, akara, boli, puff-puff, and ogi. It will provide descriptions of each dish, regional origins, key ingredients, and a flavor-preference quiz that recommends a dish based on user input. A contact/feedback form allows visitors to suggest dishes or share experiences.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • A tourist planning a trip to Lagos asks: "What street foods should I try when I visit Lagos for the first time?"
  • A Nigerian in the diaspora wonders: "Where can I find recipes and background on the street foods I grew up eating?"
  • A food blogger asks: "Which Lagos street food would best match my preference for spicy, smoky flavors?"
